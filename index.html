How to write it

Type a word in romaji (imi), hiragana or katakana (いみ, コーヒー), kanji (意味) or English (meaning).

Conjugated forms work too (tabeta, 書いて, yomikata, takakatta), and short phrases (hon wo yomimasu). Small typos are fine.

The strokes play one character after another, with numbers. Tap a character to see it big.

Ask a grammar or word question. It opens Claude with your question ready to send.
