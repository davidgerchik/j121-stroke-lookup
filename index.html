How to write it

Type a word in romaji (imi), hiragana or katakana (いみ, コーヒー), kanji (意味) or English (meaning).

The strokes play one character after another, with numbers. Tap a character to see it big.

Course words from Japanese 121 (Tobira, Lessons 1–13): taught kanji are written in kanji, kanji not taught yet are written in kana. Stroke order: KanjiVG (Ulrich Apel, CC BY-SA 3.0). Kanji readings marked "dictionary": KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0).
